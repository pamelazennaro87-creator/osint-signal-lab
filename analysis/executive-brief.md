# Executive brief — IL-2024-0001

## What open sources show
A September 2024 public DefenseML presentation documents training practices for an “Intelligence-LLM,” including a model timeline that ends with **No Spoken Arabic** after JAIS/AceGPT (Aug 2023). The same deck explains pretraining hygiene, evaluation (perplexity, few-shot, fine-tuning, human eval), ablations, and domain instruction tuning — without listing concrete intelligence tasks.

## What 2025 investigative reporting adds
Joint reporting (Guardian / +972 / Local Call) attributes a large Arabic spoken-language model effort to Unit 8200, describes intercept-derived training data at very large scale, outlines an intended analyst chatbot use-case, and notes smaller models already used in operational contexts. It also states the large model was still training in late 2024 and does not confirm deployment.

## Analytical discipline
Do not collapse “public ML methodology talk” into “proven production predictive system.”  
Do not invent task lists absent from the primary PDF.

## Bottom line
**Capability archaeology result:** construction story is public; operational task specification is not.
