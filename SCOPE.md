# Scope — osint-signal-lab

Evidence-first OSINT on publicly documented AI / intelligence capability disclosures.

## Principle
Preserve the fingerprint (public claims, timelines, primary artifacts), not stolen payloads or private personal data.

## In scope
- Public talks, slides, papers, reputable investigative reporting
- Capability-development timelines reconstructible from open sources
- Source Delta: PRIMARY vs SECONDARY vs UNRESOLVED

## Out of scope
- Private personal dossiers beyond public professional footprint
- Operational targeting advice
- Classified or leaked non-public datasets
- Unverified victim lists or raw intercept content

## Status labels
| Label | Meaning |
|-------|--------|
| PRIMARY | Stated in primary artifact (slides, video, official doc) |
| SECONDARY | Independently reported by reputable outlets with sources |
| ANALYSIS | Analyst reconstruction; not a raw fact |
| UNRESOLVED | Not demonstrated by available open sources |
